# Surface brief: marketing landing (/)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: AI-agent operators (buyers) and GPU providers (sellers), equally; judges as evaluators.
Action: open the desk (/dashboard) or run the 60-second guided demo (modal → /demo).
Proof: live testnet data (/api/testnet), real nearblocks tx links, receipt hash, verify:all. No invented numbers, customers or logos.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm, institutional exchange front page for a new asset class (GPU-hours). It refuses the crypto-landing default of neon glow, gradient orbs, bento grids and fake stats; every number on it is live from NEAR testnet or labelled as a simulation.
OWN-WORLD: White canvas, ink #0A0B0D text, one accent blue used only on primary actions and the forward-curve line, near-black hero and closing bands carrying real product cards, hairline #DEE1E6 dividers, 24px containers, pill buttons, Inter at weight 400 for display, JetBrains Mono for every number. Semantic up/down green and red only as text.
STORY: GPU prices jump, so an agent's runway breaks. Paron lets agents buy CU forwards and providers pre-sell with 25% margin. Settlement is cash at an attested index or real compute with SLA slashing. Safety (keys that cannot withdraw, guardrails, attestations) is the product. All of it is visible on testnet now.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Dark band. Left: H1 "Lock in tomorrow's GPU bill today." in 64px/400, two-line subcopy, primary pill "Open the desk", secondary pill "Run the 60-second demo". Right: layered product cards from live testnet: the CRI-H100 forward curve card (three maturities, index, bid/ask) and a smaller overlapping "order rejected by guardrail" card with its nearblocks tx. Top nav on dark with a Testnet beta status label.
FORM: Pinned by the user's brief: an exchange-grade marketing site in the minimal Coinbase reference (DESIGN.md from getdesign), with a light/dark band rhythm and layered product-UI cards. seed: none (pinned) "aku mau tampilan kita minimalis dan bagus seperti coinbase", "aku mau journey website kita seperti exchange exchange besar lainnya".
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
